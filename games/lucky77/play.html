How to play

Place bets while the countdown runs. When it hits zero, the wheel spins and the result is revealed.

  • Pick Lemon or Watermelon — not both.
  • You can always add Lucky 77 on top of either fruit.
  • Valid bets: Lemon, Watermelon, 77, Lemon + 77, or Watermelon + 77.
  • The wheel has 9 stops. Fruits pay ×2; Lucky 77 pays ×8.
