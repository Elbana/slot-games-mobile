How to play

  1. Before the timer ends, pick Home, Draw, or Away.
  2. Select a chip and tap your prediction to place your stake. You can add more chips on the same pick.
  3. Home and Away pay ×1.92. Draw pays ×3.5.
  4. Watch the live match. When full time hits, results settle and winnings are added to your balance.
  5. You can only bet on one outcome per match. Use Repeat to reuse your last stake.
